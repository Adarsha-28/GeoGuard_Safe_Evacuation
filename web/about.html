🛡️ About GeoGuard

GeoGuard is an advanced geospatial flood monitoring and prediction system designed specifically for Chennai. Using cutting-edge technology and real-time data analysis, we help protect communities by providing accurate flood risk assessments and early warning systems.

🗺️

Real-Time Monitoring

Interactive map displaying live flood risk data across 34 zones in Chennai with color-coded risk levels.

📊

Risk Assessment

Advanced algorithms analyze rainfall, elevation, and water proximity to calculate accurate flood risk scores.

🏛️

Shelter Locator

Find the nearest emergency shelters with capacity information, facilities, and contact details.

🚨

Emergency Alerts

Instant notifications for high-risk areas with actionable safety recommendations and evacuation guidance.

📞

Emergency Contacts

Quick access to 40+ emergency services including hospitals, fire stations, police, and disaster management.

📍

Location Services

GPS-based location detection to show personalized flood risk information for your current area.

🔧 Technology Stack

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript Leaflet.js GeoJSON Python OpenStreetMap SRTM Elevation Data IMD Rainfall Data

🎯 Our Mission

To provide Chennai residents with reliable, accessible, and actionable flood risk information, enabling communities to make informed decisions and stay safe during monsoon seasons and extreme weather events.
